📌*Basic HR Interview Questions*
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want this job?
- What do you know about our company?
- Why should we hire you?
- What are your strengths?
- What are your weaknesses?Are you trying to overcome that? and How?
- Explain projects or extra done internships etc..?
- Your short term and long term goal?
- Biggest achievement/ goal in life?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
- What are your salary expectations?

🔹 *Behavioral Questions*
- Tell me about a challenge you faced and how you handled it.
- Describe a situation where you worked in a team.
- Give an example of a time you showed leadership.
- Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned from it.
- How do you handle stress and pressure?

🔹 *Job & Skill-Based Questions*
- What skills do you bring to this role?
- Tell me about a project you are proud of?
- How do you prioritize your tasks?
- How quickly can you adapt to new tools/technology?

🔹 *Cultural Fit Questions*
- What kind of work environment do you prefer?
- How do you handle conflicts at work?
- How do you respond to feedback?
- What motivates you at work?

🔹 *Situational Questions*
- If your manager gives sudden urgent work, how will you manage it?
- If you disagree with your manager, what will you do?
- What will you do if you feel overloaded with tasks?

🔹 *Ending Questions*
- Do you have any questions for us?
- When can you join?
